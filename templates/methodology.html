RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Proposed Methodology

Quality-aware patient-specific multimodal fusion of ECG and clinical data.

01

PTB-XL Dataset

Read 12-lead ECG signals, diagnostic labels and available patient metadata.

02

ECG Preprocessing

Validate signals, handle recording artefacts and prepare ECG inputs.

03

Clinical Preprocessing

Address missing values and prepare structured patient information.

04

Independent Encoders

Proposed: 1D-CNN or Transformer for ECG; MLP or XGBoost for clinical features.

05

Quality Assessment

Estimate ECG signal reliability and completeness of metadata for each patient.

06

Adaptive Fusion

Investigate learned patient-specific reliability weights instead of fixed fusion.

07

Uncertainty Estimation

Evaluate calibration and uncertainty to identify predictions needing caution.

08

Explainable AI

Investigate ECG signal attribution and clinical-feature importance.

ARCHITECTURE

Proposed end-to-end flow

PTB-XL ECG + metadata
↓
ECG branchPreprocess → 1D-CNN / Transformer
Clinical branchMissing values → MLP / XGBoost
↓
Quality assessment → patient-specific reliability → adaptive fusion
↓
Diagnostic prediction + uncertainty + explanations
Implementation distinction: The website's executable baseline currently uses statistical ECG features, logistic regression and heuristic quality weighting. The proposed neural encoders, learned gating, calibrated uncertainty and attribution methods are not yet implemented.
